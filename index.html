QUICK GUIDE

Control the map with your hand

Open palm: Move your hand horizontally to rotate the map.

Pinch: Move thumb and index finger together or apart to zoom.

Point: Extend your index finger and point at the map to select a location.

Good lighting and a clear view of one hand help tracking. You can always use your mouse and keyboard too.
